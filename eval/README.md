# eval

Benchmarks, each in its own folder with the script that produced it and the
committed result. A number quoted anywhere in the repo links to a file here.

| Folder | What | Rerun |
|---|---|---|
| `markout_sol_10s/` | SOL maker-fill markout model (the deployed v2 model): purged k-fold CV on May 27 to 31, 2026, forward holdout June 1 | Purged k-fold was removed from the trainer (v3 validates with the rolling daily walk-forward only); at commit `7035c0c`, with the local Gold partitions: `python -m marketimmune.cli.train --coin SOL --dates 20260527..20260531 --holdout-date 20260601 --horizon 10s --iterations 150 --n-splits 5 --cv-scheme purged_kfold` |
| `sol_dec_registered/` | The pre-registered SOL December 2025 ladder (`docs/preregistration/sol-dec-2025-ladder.md`) as first run: classifier and quantile reports, rung comparisons, MCS, policies, decomposition, forecaster evaluation. OOF parquet files are gitignored | Code `7035c0c`, Dec 12 excluded; see the pre-registration |
| `sol_dec_corrected/` | The same ladder on the month rebuilt after the 2026-10-31 data fixes (pre-registration, Deviations) | `powershell -ExecutionPolicy Bypass -File .\scripts\run_ladder.ps1 -Coin SOL -Name sol_dec -Out eval\sol_dec_corrected` |
| `ladder_verdicts.json` | H1-H5 verdicts for both SOL runs under the pre-registered rules (`docs/research/sol-dec-2025-ladder-results.md`) | `python -m marketimmune.cli.score_ladder --runs eval/sol_dec_registered,eval/sol_dec_corrected` |
| `eth_dec/` | The confirmatory ETH December 2025 ladder (`docs/preregistration/eth-dec-2025-ladder.md`): the same reports plus `ladder_verdicts.json`; results in `docs/research/eth-dec-2025-ladder-results.md` | `powershell -ExecutionPolicy Bypass -File .\scripts\run_ladder.ps1 -Coin ETH -Name eth_dec` |
| `sim_sol.json` | Block-accurate maker PnL on SOL December: S1, the stale-quote rule and (with `--signals-dir`) S2/S3 injected into the rebuilt book, both queue models, PnL split by component (`docs/research/block-simulator.md`) | `python -m marketimmune.cli.build_albers_silver --coin SOL --dates 20251201..20251231 --mbo`, then `python -m marketimmune.cli.simulate_quotes --coin SOL --signals-dir data/gold/hyperliquid/quote_signals --out eval/sim_sol.json` |
| `sim_frontier_sol.json`, `sim_frontier_eth.json` | S2 and S3 at pull limits of 0.5-5 bps against a time-shifted S2 placebo, Dec 8-31 (exploratory; `docs/research/block-simulator.md`) | `python -m marketimmune.cli.pull_frontier --coin SOL --dates 20251208..20251231 --out eval/sim_frontier_sol.json`, and `--coin ETH` (days cached; `--max-days` per call) |
| `sim_hft_sol.json` | S1 in hftbacktest (risk-averse, power-probability queues) against the engine, with and without the L2 crossing rule, SOL December, per day and for the month (`docs/research/block-simulator.md`) | `.venv-sim` (extra `sim`): `python -m marketimmune.cli.hft_baseline --coin SOL --dates 20251201..20251231 --out eval/sim_hft_sol.json` (days cached; `--max-days` per call) |
| `node_fills_reconciliation.json` | Node fills (`pull_node_fills`) against the Albers Silver fills, trade by trade, SOL and ETH December: matches, field differences, one-sided trades and the archive's missing windows (`docs/research/node-fills-reconciliation.md`) | `python -m marketimmune.cli.reconcile_node_fills --coins SOL,ETH --dates 20251201..20251231` |
| `priority_fee_bands.json` | Priority fees in BTC, ETH and SOL fills, Mar to Oct 2026: rollout date, monthly fee-paying share, fee quantiles, the units check, and maker markout by the taker's fee band with day-level intervals, since rollout and for the last 60 days (`docs/research/priority-fees.md`) | `python -m marketimmune.cli.pull_candles --coin HYPE --intervals 1h,4h`, then `python -m marketimmune.cli.priority_fee_bands --coins BTC,ETH,SOL --dates 20260301..20261031` and the same with `--report eval/priority_fee_bands.json` |
| `trade_mid_validation.json` | Markouts on the trade-implied mid against Gold's book-based ones, December 2025 (SOL month, ETH Dec 1-15) | `python -m marketimmune.cli.validate_trade_mids --coins SOL,ETH --dates 20251201..20251231` |
| `position_book_validation.json` | The forecaster's position book and margin-free liquidation prior against Hydromancer's 17 snapshots (Dec 15-31, SOL and ETH), and forced fills against the latest snapshot's liquidation prices (`docs/research/position-book-validation.md`) | `python -m marketimmune.cli.validate_positions --coins SOL,ETH --dates 20251201..20251231` |
| `a1/` | The pre-registered A1 ladder (priority fees as a toxicity signal, BTC/ETH/SOL, Apr 25 to Oct 31 2026; rules in `docs/preregistration/a1-speed-bids-2026.md`): per-rung OOF predictions and summaries, rung comparisons and MCS per period under `discovery/` and `confirmation/`, and the verdicts in `a1_verdicts.json` (`a1_discovery_exploratory.json` is the same scoring on discovery, not a test); secondary analyses in `secondary/`, `lead_time.json` and `h1s/`, `h60s/`; every table in `a1_tables.md` (`docs/research/a1-results.md`) | `powershell -ExecutionPolicy Bypass -File .\scripts\run_a1.ps1`, then `.\scripts\run_a1_secondary.ps1` (`-WithHorizons` for 1 s and 60 s) |
| `forced_v2_sol.json`, `forced_v2_eth.json` | Forced-flow forecaster v2 (exploratory): v1's prior, v2a (snapshot prices while untraded) and v2 (snapshot prices plus the account roll) on node-fill inputs, December 2025; each variant on its own days, v1 on v2's days, and paired comparisons (`docs/research/forecaster-v2.md`) | `python -m marketimmune.cli.forced_v2 --coin SOL --dates 20251201..20251231` until done, then the same with `--report eval/forced_v2_sol.json`; ETH likewise |
| `forced_v2_checks.json` | The account roll against the next snapshot, first liquidations against snapshot prices by method and margin (the backstop's 2/3 threshold), and the 2025-10-10 crash: snapshot vs realised liquidations and the forecasts minute by minute (`docs/research/forecaster-v2.md`) | `python -m marketimmune.cli.forced_v2 --coin SOL --dates 20251008..20251012 --out-dir data/gold/hyperliquid/forced_v2_oct` (and ETH), then `python -m marketimmune.cli.forced_v2_checks --coins SOL,ETH --out eval/forced_v2_checks.json` |
| `quote_signals_eth.json`, `sim_eth.json` | The same for ETH December: quote-time S2/S3 models and the simulator run with them (`docs/research/block-simulator.md`) | `powershell -ExecutionPolicy Bypass -File .\scripts\run_eth_sim.ps1` |
| `quote_signals_sol.json` | Quote-time S2/S3 models for the simulator: per-model pinball skill on the Gold fills they did not train on, the dropped fill-time columns, and per day the train/serve check, unscored share and pull share at 2 bps (`docs/research/block-simulator.md`) | `python -m marketimmune.cli.quote_signals --coin SOL --dates 20251201..20251231 --report eval/quote_signals_sol.json` |
| `pipeline_speed/` | Stage timings on a synthetic panel shaped like the SOL run | `python eval/pipeline_speed/bench.py --out eval/pipeline_speed/results.json` |

The Rust replay benchmark is `cargo bench -p hl-replay --bench replay`
(`crates/hl-replay/benches/replay.rs`); its numbers are in `docs/performance.md`.
