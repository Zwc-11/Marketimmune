## Why these markets

**On the nine majors the fee is the binding constraint.**
- Every quoter tested broke even at a maker fee of 0.2–1.0 bp. The account pays 1.5 bp.
- The quiet-depth maker (`hush@`, `paper-hush-20261107.md`) showed two things:
  - depth helps (d = 1 / 2 / 3 bp: −$18.7 / −$5.2 / +$1.1 an hour);
  - A1's wallet gate helps.

**Hyperliquid's builder-deployed (HIP-3) markets in growth mode charge 0.3 bp maker and 0.9 bp
taker at tier 0** (docs.trade.xyz/perp-mechanics/fees: "Growth mode: Taker 0.0090%, Maker 0.0030%").
- They are trade.xyz equities, indices, commodities and FX, and pre-IPO synthetic markets (xyz, io).
- Several trade $10–74M a day.
- They have no Coinbase product. Each one's oracle (`activeAssetCtx.oraclePx`) is the engine's price
  reference.
- **Assumption, to check before any real order:** the EntropyIO (`io:`) markets charge the same
  growth-mode rates as trade.xyz's. Both dexes report `growthMode: enabled` and
  `deployerFeeScale: 1.0`.

## Data and code

**Recording.** `scripts/record_hip3.py`, run in the cloud workspace from 2026-11-08 00:22 UTC. It
records `bbo`, `trades` (with users) and `activeAssetCtx` for 32 growth-mode markets
(`eval/paper/hip3/coins.txt`), in the paper recorder's raw format. The data are not in git; the
replays are reproducible from the raw files. `xyz:GOLD` was recorded but is excluded, because it is
not in growth mode.

**Engine changes (a1a1278, e0f69f5).**
- the oracle is the reference for markets named `dex:NAME`;
- dex-prefixed coin names and HIP-3 price steps;
- `paper_tune --coins`;
- `--twins` (not used here);
- retimed replays keep oracle updates.

**Replay settings.**
- maker 0.3 bp, taker 0.9 bp;
- $250 × 4 quotes a market, $5,000 budget per strategy, 200 ms order latency;
- receive times as recorded (cloud clock, about 250 ms behind block time);
- wallet scores start cold.

## What a pass would and would not show

**A pass would show** that deep quoting on these markets beat the fee on unseen weekend hours, at
this engine's queue and latency model.

**It would not show:**
- **Weekdays.** The selection and test are both weekend hours, when the equity oracles are still
  and the pre-IPO markets trade on their own. Weekday hours, when the oracles follow live stock
  prices, need their own test.
- **Real fills.** Paper fills don't move the book and can't be stepped in front of.
- **Capacity beyond $250 quotes.**
- **That the EntropyIO fee assumption holds.**
