# Pre-registration: a market maker with inventory and risk control, 7 days live on paper (from 2026-11-06)

Written 2026-11-06 at about 22:00 UTC, before the run it governs starts. Drafted by Claude at
Caesar's request ("market making ... very high speed ... stop loss ... portfolio management ... so
let's do it"). The commit time is the registration time; the run starts after it.

## Why

The gap quoters (`paper/strategies.py`) sit at the touch and stop a side only at a coin's position
limit. Their PnL is mostly the position they hold times the market's move, so it moves against BTC
whenever they are net short. `paper/mm.py` is a textbook market maker instead:

- it quotes around a fair price (mid, microprice, Coinbase lead-lag forecast);
- it leans its quotes to work each coin's position and the net dollars back to zero;
- it has a risk layer: a stop-loss per coin, a kill switch on drawdown, a net cap, burst pulls, and a
  dead man's switch on silent feeds.

Replayed on the 8.19 hours the tuned run was picked on, with 0.94 hour after them held out
(`eval/paper/mm_20261106.{json,md}`), it loses at every setting. The spread it earns is smaller than
the move against it within a second of each fill. The pick within Hyperliquid's rate limit is
`mm@h=2/skew=6`: -$12.7 an hour in-sample, -$14.9 left-one-segment-out, and -$11.4 on the held-out
hour. Every setting loses in a Tokyo replay too, and at a zero maker fee. This run tests the
prediction live.

## Run

- **Where:** Caesar's machine, `scripts/run_paper_mm.ps1`, beside the 24-hour and tuned runs (port
  8767): `cli.paper_trade --hours 168 --segment-hours 2 --clock coinbase --quote-usd 250
  --max-quotes 4 --budget-usd 5000`, the model state carried between segments, the decision
  journal on.
- **Fixed:** the nine coins; 200 ms order latency on the corrected clock; 1.5 bp maker and 4.5 bp
  taker fee; the fill model and the strategies' code at this commit.
- **Length:**
  - 7 days (168 hours) of live data after the start;
  - hours lost to sleep, crashes or feed outages are excluded, reported, and made up by continuing,
    up to 14 days from the start;
  - at 14 days the test is scored on what exists if it is at least 3 days; under 3 days there is no
    verdict.
- Looking at the live view during the run is allowed. It does not stop or extend the run.

## Hypothesis

- **Registered:** `mm@h=2/skew=6`, summed over the nine coins.
- **Prediction:** it loses money. The replays put it at -$11 to -$15 an hour.
- **Rule:** the stationary bootstrap interval of its mean 15-minute block PnL (mean block 3, 5,000
  draws, seed 0) at 95%:
  - entirely below zero: the prediction holds; this market maker, at the base fee and this PC's
    latency, is closed;
  - entirely above zero: the replay is wrong about it; the fill model is examined before anything
    else;
  - otherwise: no verdict.
- **Also reported, no claim:**
  - its PnL split into spread, the move within 1 s of each fill, fees and the rest
    (`cli.paper_report`);
  - the exits (stops, kill switch) and orders per USD filled;
  - against `zgap@0.05` and `touch` on the same blocks.

## Exploratory, no claims

- `mm@1.5`: the default settings.
- `mm@3`: the setting closest to break-even in the replays (-$6.5 an hour), but at 1.9 orders per
  USD filled it is over Hyperliquid's rate limit.
- `mm@h=1.5/stop=0/dd=0/net=0/burst=0`: the defaults with every risk control off.
- `zgap@0.05` (one of the tuned run's candidates, here at the same sizing) and `touch` (control).

## What will not be done

- No change to the strategies, settings, coins or fill model during the run. A bug fix that changes
  fills would end the test and be recorded here.
- No stopping or extending the run because of its results.
- No claim about real orders. Paper fills are not real fills.
